# Datasets: A Flexible Modelling of Extremes in the Presence of Inliers

This repository contains the two real-world datasets used in the paper **“A Flexible Modelling of Extremes in the Presence of Inliers”**, published in the *Journal of Statistical Computation and Simulation* (2026).

## Datasets

### Dataset 1:  **Syphilis Dataset:** Weekly syphilis cases in Maryland, USA (2007–2010), from the `ZIM` package in R.

### Dataset 2:  **Pune Rainfall Dataset:** Monthly rainfall observations from Pune, Maharashtra, India (1965–2002).


## Reference

Nila, S., Das, I., & Balakrishna, N. (2026). A flexible modelling of extremes in the presence of inliers. *Journal of Statistical Computation and Simulation*.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/00949655.2026.2752400
